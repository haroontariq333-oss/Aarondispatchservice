The 4 checks we run before we book any load

Broker credit checks

A top rate from a broker who pays in 60 days, or never, isn't a top rate.

We check credit and days-to-pay before we book.

True RPM

$3,000 for 1,000 loaded miles is $3.00/mi. Add 200 deadhead miles and it's $2.50/mi.

We price every load on total miles.

Outbound markets

A big rate into a dead market can mean a cheap reload or a long deadhead out.

We check what's moving out of the drop city first.

Detention terms

Hours at the dock don't pay unless the rate con says so.

We get free time and the hourly detention rate in writing before you roll.

FAQ

Questions drivers ask us

What does the 5% cover?

Everything: load searching, rate negotiation, broker setup packets, rate cons, check calls and sending invoices to your factoring company. There are no setup fees, weekly fees or extra charges, and we never take a cut of detention or layover.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No. We work load by load and you can cancel anytime. We'd rather keep you because the loads pay.

Will you force me to take loads?

Never. We send you the details: lane, miles, rate, RPM, pickup and delivery times. Nothing is booked until you say yes.

Will I see the real rate the broker pays?

Yes. You receive the original rate confirmation from the broker, so you always know exactly what the load pays and exactly what our 5% is.

I just got my authority. Can you still help?

Yes, new authorities are one of our main focuses. Many brokers won't take carriers under 90 days, so we target the brokers and load boards that accept new MCs and build your history from there.

What do I need to get started?

Your W9, MC certificate and certificate of insurance (COI). If you use a factoring company, send their details too and we'll invoice them directly.

How does the first week at 0% work?

For new clients, we don't charge our fee on loads you haul during your first week with us. After that it's 5% flat.
